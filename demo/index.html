Handwired Brain Transformer

Weight-space-programmed 3D transformers (no training) segmenting DANDI 000108 light-sheet human brain, streamed chunk by chunk from the OME-Zarr pyramid and run on WebGPU.

Drag to pan, wheel to zoom. In Auto the model and pyramid level follow the zoom: tissue when zoomed out, cells (YO/NN) or vessels (LEC) when zoomed in.
